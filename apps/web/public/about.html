Your agents, connected to your tools.

Agent Control is a product by JemAI Labs. Use your Hermes agents from your computer or phone, manage conversations and connect the services you choose.

Open Agent Control

Connect your own email

In Settings → Plugins, choose Gmail or Outlook. You sign in on the provider’s own page, select your mailbox and authorize access. You can connect several accounts and choose which of your agents may use each one. Hostinger and other email services use a secure IMAP/SMTP connection.

You control access

Your connected accounts belong to your Agent Control user. An agent can access only the accounts you assign to it. Drafting prepares text in the conversation; sending requires your explicit instruction. New connections are available in a new conversation, with your computer online.

How email is processed

Agent Control stores connection credentials encrypted and processes requested email to pass it to your selected agent and its configured AI provider. Email used by an agent may appear in conversation history and caches. This is not end-to-end encryption. Disconnect an account in Plugins to withdraw the agent’s access. For questions about access or deletion of cloud data, contact support.

Gmail access is currently limited to registered beta testers. Organization accounts may require administrator approval.
